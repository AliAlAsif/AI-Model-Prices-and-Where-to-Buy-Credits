Before you buy

  • Most providers sell prepaid credits that expire after about a year and are non-refundable. Buy only what you will use, and start with the minimum.
  • Turn auto-reload off unless you want automatic purchases. OpenAI turns it on by default during setup.
  • Supported countries, accepted cards, local payment methods and taxes (VAT or GST) differ by provider, so your final cost can be noticeably above the credit amount.
  • Watch for changing prices: promotions end, and DeepSeek charges double at peak hours. AUTO rows pick up list-price changes by themselves, but the notes beside them are written by hand.
  • An aggregator such as OpenRouter lets one balance reach many models, at the cost of a purchase fee.
  • Output tokens usually cost 3 to 6 times input tokens, so a model with cheap input can still be expensive for long answers. Use the cost column with your own token counts.
